# INBOOK — Concept and Tools

## 1. Project Overview

INBOOK is a hotel discovery and comparison platform focused on hotels in Egypt. It aims to bring hotel information together in one place, helping users explore available options and compare hotels based on important details such as location, price, rating, and available facilities.

The project focuses on improving the visibility of hotels, including smaller properties, and making hotel information easier for travelers to access.

## 2. Problem Statement

Finding and comparing hotels can be challenging for travelers because hotel information is often distributed across multiple platforms.

Some of the main challenges include:

- Difficulty comparing hotel prices and details across different sources.
- Scattered information about hotel locations, ratings, and facilities.
- Limited online visibility for smaller hotels.
- High commission fees associated with some traditional booking platforms.

## 3. Proposed Solution

INBOOK aims to provide a centralized platform where users can explore and compare hotels in Egypt.

The proposed solution includes:

- Displaying hotel information in one place.
- Allowing users to compare hotels based on price, location, ratings, and facilities.
- Improving the visibility of small and independent hotels.
- Providing a simpler way for travelers to discover suitable accommodation options.

The current project version focuses on hotel listing, information display, and comparison as a demonstration of the platform concept.

## 4. Target Users

INBOOK is designed to serve three main user groups:

### Customers / Travelers
Users who want to explore hotels, review their details, and compare available options.

### Hotel Owners / Managers
Hotel representatives who may benefit from increased visibility and the opportunity to present their properties to potential customers.

### System Administrators
Users responsible for managing platform information and maintaining the quality of the available hotel data.

## 5. Business Model

INBOOK explores a business model designed to reduce the financial burden associated with traditional hotel booking platforms.

The proposed model includes:

- A points-based wallet system for customers.
- A direct connection between customers and hotels.
- A proposed 10% platform commission, with the remaining 90% going to the hotel.

This model represents the project's business concept and future direction. These commercial features are not considered implemented functionality in the current demonstration version.
